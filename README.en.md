# NBA

Each night's NBA games in Paris time, with live scores, box scores and advanced stats, both conference standings, a page for each team, player profiles and notifications for your teams' games. Data comes from ESPN.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

## Games

- The **Games** tab shows the current night; the arrows move to the **previous** or **next night**, and **Tonight** comes back to it.
- Each game shows both teams and their record, the start time (Paris time), the score, the quarter and time left when live, and the top scorers. During a game, scores update every 30 seconds.
- Your teams' games come first.

## Box score

Click a game to open it:

- the **score by quarter**;
- **team stats**: rebounds (including offensive), assists, steals, blocks, turnovers, points in the paint, on fast breaks and off turnovers, largest lead, fouls;
- **advanced stats**: effective shooting (eFG %, a three counts as 1.5), true shooting (TS %, free throws included), points per 100 possessions, pace (estimated possessions), assisted baskets, assists per turnover, turnovers per possession;
- **each player's line**: minutes, points, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, shots and threes; starters are in bold. It appears at tip-off.

## Players

Click a player for their profile: position, photo, regular season, playoff and career averages, and their latest games, each leading to its box score.

## Standings

The **Standings** tab gives the Eastern and Western conferences: record, win percentage, games behind the leader and current streak. Places 1 to 6 go to the playoffs, 7 to 10 to the play-in.

## Teams

Click a team for its page: its record, upcoming games and latest results.

A team's **star** makes it one of your teams: its games come first, can go into your schedule and trigger notifications.

## Schedule and notifications

- Tick **Add my favourite teams' games to my schedule**: their games go into the NeoDex calendar and are updated every 3 hours.
- Notifications, to turn on in NeoDex settings:
  - **game**: one of your teams' games starts within the hour;
  - **result**: the final score of one of your teams' games.

  They are checked every quarter of an hour, even when the NBA tab is closed.

## Data and sync

Your favourite teams and schedule choice are stored by NeoDex, backed up and synced between your PCs when sync is on. On first launch, the plugin picks up what you already had in NeoDex. Scores and statistics are read live from ESPN.

## Install

In NeoDex: **NeoStore → Discover → NBA → Install**.

Building a plugin: see the [NeoDex SDK](https://github.com/NDesumeur/neodex-sdk).
